# Project Context

## Overview

dalio-machine is the canonical data and reasoning engine behind the user-facing **Macro History & Risk Observatory**. It is expanding from a macro-cycle dashboard into one point-in-time history of economic observations, institutional reports and communications, debt, large-allocator positions, financial flows, risk pathways and their implications for a SEK-based household investor. The existing product covers 8 cycle economies (US, CN, EU, UK, JP, SE, IN, BR), pulls macro indicators from FRED, BIS, Riksbank, SCB, IMF, OECD and World Bank, and classifies cycle stage with transparent rules.

Since slice 18 it also hosts the **World Fundamentals Map**: 21 countries + the euro-area aggregate scored by percentile on fundamentals across five first-principles categories (real stuff · production · exchange · promises · enforcer), with purpose-specific *views* (jurisdiction gate for concentrated stock positions, allocation lens, learning, moonshot geography) as weight vectors over the category scores. Design in `decisions/0001-fundamentals-map.md`; full plan in `~/.claude/plans/radiant-bubbling-micali.md`.

The framework is treated as a descriptive lens, not a predictive oracle. The product surfaces state, constraints, alternative pathways and portfolio fragilities with explicit evidence and confidence — it does not output market-timing or automatic buy/sell signals. `dalio-machine` is the sole upstream macro source; Börsdata/company analysis consumes versioned exports and never writes verdicts back into the macro fact base (ADR 0004).

## Conventions

- **Naming:** snake_case for files/functions/vars; PascalCase for classes; UPPER_CASE for module-level constants. Indicator names are snake_case strings (`cpi_yoy`, `policy_rate`).
- **File Organization:** Tests mirror src tree (`tests/test_<module>.py`). One module per data source / indicator family.
- **Error Handling:** Pipelines log + collect failures per series; never let one bad series fail the whole batch. Tests use mocked HTTP — never real API calls.
- **Imports:** Absolute imports from `dalio.*`. No relative imports.
- **Time-series invariants:** All observations are long-format `(country, indicator, date, value, source, series_id)`. Reference periods use `date`; release clocks are UTC-normalized timestamps. `observations` has no duplicate `(country, indicator, date, source)` rows. Point-in-time history is append-only, content-idempotent, stores complete independently replaceable partitions, and selects the latest release by `available_at` before filtering its rows. Unknown availability is conservatively set to retrieval time, never inferred from the reference period.
- **Market-history invariants:** commodity blanks, spreadsheet errors and nonpositive price/index cells remain missing and spot/reference prices are not relabelled as futures returns. Commodity workbooks must retain their expected catalogue, 1960-01 start, monthly cadence and recency and publish as one atomic group; a stored contraction needs a reviewed override. Broad money, central-bank assets, policy rates, yields, offshore credit, MMF assets, repo stocks/volumes/rates and FX remain different concepts; native M2/M3/M4/`L` definitions, currencies and monthly/quarterly/weekly/daily frequencies are not collapsed into one “money” level. The UK quarterly M4ex bridge and its monthly parent overlap without becoming two votes. Money and frontier histories must retain pinned starts and cadence, remain within source-specific publication lags, cannot contract without a reviewed override, and require intact full-hash release artifacts for readiness. Publisher nulls remain missing; selected repo venues cannot be presented as the whole market or as a sector-to-sector cash trace.
- **Derived-liquidity invariants:** there is no composite liquidity, risk, M5 or investment score. Primary currencies stay separate; unlike stocks, rates and transaction volumes are never summed. Broad-money median/breadth requires all five primary readings at one common period. Release selection precedes the economic-date cap, exact calendar lags are required and publisher gaps are neither forward-filled nor zero-filled. MMF-versus-M2 growth is not a deposit-flow measure; published repo counterparty/clearing-category ratios are non-additive, FICC is a clearing category and they do not reveal an end-to-end cash map. Repo venue dispersion is not a pure credit spread and money-versus-central-bank-assets growth does not establish causal transmission. Negative offshore-credit growth is arithmetic contraction and a negative change is deceleration, not a claim of tightness. Pre-full-cutover history in the selected liquidity releases is `current_vintage_history`, not a known-at-the-time backtest.
- **Typed-snapshot invariants:** debt-holder and allocator dimensions remain columns rather than encoded indicator names. Their point-in-time queries select the latest complete eligible release before filtering cells, so a newer omission cannot resurrect an older holder or line item. Publisher non-reporting, rounding and non-additivity remain visible rather than being repaired by inference.
- **Report-evidence invariants:** original PDF bytes are content-addressed; source identity and official domains come from the trusted manifest; extractors must return every physical page in 1-based order; facts, forecasts and judgments remain publisher-attributed while inferences are Observatory-attributed; page location is mechanically checked, semantic support is human-reviewed, and verification/supersession append rows rather than mutate history. Decision files bind the rebuilt full packet/catalogue hashes and contain neither reviewer nor clock; all candidates must be decided before a backed-up, atomic apply whose CLI shows every candidate/outcome and confirms the canonical decision hash. A local `human:` identity is attribution rather than cryptographic authentication, and the human-only rule is operating policy.
- **Communication-evidence invariants:** the source-policy catalogue is discovery metadata, never download permission. Event identity is distinct from each representation; artifact-level host, historical publisher, transcriber, origin, language, rights and three clocks remain explicit. Immutable link metadata and retrieval-bound byte captures are separate, so later rights clearance or changed bytes at one URL cannot rewrite history. Every v2 artifact has one hash-bound ordered scope set before retrieval; mixed representations keep one byte lineage while prepared remarks, elicited Q&A, official captions, automatic captions and local speech-to-text are never flattened into one provenance tier. Segment ordinals and kinds must match those scopes, and inventory independently rechecks the binding and clocks. Corrections append versions; page/paragraph/timecode locator endpoints are paired; questions cannot silently become publisher claims; commodity mappings use stable lineage keys and remain effective-dated selection taxonomy until evidence and human review exist. No complete-archive, sentiment, causality, forecast, risk or portfolio conclusion follows from catalogue or document counts.

## Cycle Stage Classification

Rule-based, transparent, four stages per Dalio's roadmap (slice 1 — short-term cycle). Each rule emits one or more `StageVote(stage, weight, reason)`. Stage = highest summed weight; "Transition" if top two stages within 0.3 weight units. Confidence is saturating: `top_weight / (total_weight + 1.0)` so a single weak vote never reads as 100%.

| Stage | Trigger conditions |
|-------|--------------------|
| 1. Expansion | `real_gdp_yoy > 1.5` AND `cpi_yoy < 3` AND unemployment stable/falling. Steep yield curve adds weight. |
| 2. Inflationary peak | `cpi_yoy > 4` (strong) OR `cpi_yoy > 3` with CB tightening (moderate) OR CPI accelerating from ≥2.5% (modifier). |
| 3. Recession | `real_gdp_yoy < 0` OR `unemployment_change_3m > 0.5pp` (Sahm-rule territory). Inverted yield curve adds weight. |
| 4. Reflation | CB cutting strongly (>0.5pp/6m) OR moderately (0.25–0.5pp/6m) with `cpi_yoy < 4` OR CPI decelerating sharply. |

All thresholds in `src/dalio/scoring/short_term.py`. Vote weights and reasons are exposed in the dashboard (expandable "Rule reasoning") so every classification is inspectable.

ADR 0017 caps further foundation collection at five packages. The 16-event 2025

core-economy debt maturity/refinancing package now has a fixed checked
denominator of 48 logical streams: 31 harmonized Eurostat/ECB scalar partitions
are source-ready but not ingested, and 17 national-native streams are still
planned. The package is therefore not complete. It is followed by comparable historical
pension- and sovereign-fund allocations (about 40–80 annual snapshots);
cross-border banking, collateral, FX-swap and funding-stress evidence (about
20–40 series); and selected historical bank/commodity-company communication
metadata (about 100–250 records). These are planning bounds, not quotas or a
completeness score. Unavailable or incomparable first-party evidence remains an
explicit gap. When these packages are completed or deliberately closed, the
project pivots to Slice 35B rather than expanding the warehouse. Communication
content remains outside Version 1 unless separately rights-cleared and
semantically reviewed.

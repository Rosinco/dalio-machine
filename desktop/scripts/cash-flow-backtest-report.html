Compare forecasts with the annual cash flows that followed, find the misses, and separate model errors from changes in the underlying data.

Offline research report. The installed 0.13.0 starter remains an editable sensitivity model. These results do not change saved valuations or establish investment returns.

Data vintage matters. Historical provider FCF changes between downloads are widespread, including cases where operating and investing cash did not change. The frozen-snapshot result measures cross-vintage proxy agreement; it cannot isolate forecasting skill. Why the snapshot result needs a data-quality qualification

Provider vintage changes may represent definition changes, corrections or revisions. Their causes have not been established. They must be reconciled before attributing cross-vintage misses to business unpredictability. The ledger retains the full frozen cohort, including listings missing from the later directory; disappearance is not treated as bankruptcy.

Make the next forecast more informative

  1. Reconcile the cash definition. Store operating cash, maintenance and growth investment, acquisitions and disposals separately. Financial firms need suitable cash and capital models.
  2. Test the mid forecast separately from the band. Keep simple baselines and compare models on the same companies and years. A wider range is not evidence of a better mid forecast.
  3. Learn errors by horizon and relevant peer group. Use pooled history and sufficient samples; keep an absolute cash scale so a near-zero forecast does not imply certainty. Test future changes on a new untouched period.
  4. Track causes, dates and revisions. Give each miss a review record: data issue, working capital, investment cycle, acquisition/disposal, cycle shift or unexplained. Distinguish what was knowable at the forecast date from later explanations.
  5. Freeze forecasts going forward. Save the input snapshot, universe, assumptions and forecast when created, then score against subsequent reports. Keep deep-dive revisions alongside the original baseline.
Audit protocol and limitations

    Methods: rolling-origin evaluation; proper interval scoring. This audit evaluates nominal annual cash proxies, not the accuracy of terminal values, DCF prices, discount rates or stock returns.
